# Third-party sounds

The key sounds in `app/src/main/res/raw/` are cut from four packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), all released into the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required; we give it anyway. Each file was cut to at most 0.25 s (the longer ones faded out and their trailing silence removed), mixed to mono and saved as 22.05 kHz 16-bit WAV. The Glass, Wood, Retro and Chips files also had their leading silence removed and were raised to full level. See ADR 0008.
